# Beach Rescue: 3D Animated Simulation for Lifesaving Education and Emergency Response

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **3D animated beach rescue simulation** designed to support **lifesaving education and emergency response awareness**. The simulation visualizes a complete rescue chain in a coastal environment, starting from the early detection of a drowning victim and ending with medical evacuation by ambulance.

The project was developed using **Blender** and emphasizes realism, environmental challenges, and accurate rescue procedures to demonstrate the complexity of real-world emergency response scenarios.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Visualize realistic **beach rescue operations**
- Demonstrate the impact of **rip currents, crowd congestion, and traffic delays**
- Highlight the importance of **rapid response, teamwork, and medical intervention**
- Use **3D animation** as an educational tool for public safety and training

## 🧩 Project Structure
The animation is organized into **eight sequential scenes**, each focusing on specific technical and narrative elements:

1. **Victim Detection** – Lifeguard observes the drowning victim
2. **Crowded Beach Response** – Lifeguard navigates through people
3. **Water Rescue** – Swimming against strong waves and rip currents
4. **Traffic Congestion** – Ambulance delayed on the road
5. **CPR on Shore** – Basic life support intervention
6. **Ambulance Arrival** – Reduced visibility and environmental effects
7. **Defibrillation** – Advanced medical intervention
8. **Evacuation** – Patient transported into the ambulance

---

## 🌊 Simulation & Technical Features
- Physics-based **ocean simulation** using Ocean Modifier
- Character–water interaction via **Dynamic Paint**
- Manual keyframe animation for rescue and CPR accuracy
- Particle systems for **dust and wind effects**
- Consistent lighting using **Sun Light**
- Camera motion designed to support storytelling and urgency

---

## 📊 Results & Findings
- Rescue efficiency was strongly affected by:
  - **Crowd density**
  - **Sea currents**
  - **Traffic congestion**
- Environmental and logistical challenges had a **cumulative impact** on rescue timing and medical outcomes
- The simulation effectively demonstrates how delays and hazards influence emergency response
